# Impeccable Design Context — كريم معرفة | Knowledge Kareem

## Design Context

### Users
Employees of a Saudi **media production group** (multiple companies) — producers, editors, creatives, technical and admin staff. They arrive from an internal announcement email or a WhatsApp link, mostly on phones, and are deciding in under a minute whether to register interest in an internal knowledge-sharing initiative. They are *motion-literate professionals*: they cut film, grade footage, and design title sequences for a living. Cheap animation reads as amateurism to them; cinematic craft reads as respect.

### Brand Personality
From the client brief: احترافي (professional) · ملهم (inspiring) · ذكي (smart) · حديث (modern) · راقٍ (refined) · إنساني (human).
Three working words: **cinematic, metallic, generous**. The initiative's core metaphor: knowledge starts as a single dot of light; shared, it becomes a network. Official tagline: «شارك المعرفة.. واصنع الأثر».

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Minimal Luxury / Modern Corporate** — WEF, McKinsey, HBR annual-report grade, executed like a production house's own brand reel. NOT an edu platform, student club, or training course.
- Fixed palette (client brief): Deep Navy `#0B1220` primary; Silver Metallic `#C9CED6`; Light Silver `#E6EAF0`; White; Electric Silver `#A8B3C4` accent (sparing). Status colors are desaturated oxblood/viridian — the only chroma on the page.
- Fixed typography (client brief): IBM Plex Sans Arabic (AR) / IBM Plex Sans (EN), weights 400/500/600 only. Never letter-space Arabic. (Client-mandated — overrides generic font-variety guidance.)
- Dark/light is per-section: dark navy "cover" sections, white/light-silver "content" sections, dark close.
- **Forbidden imagery** (client brief, applies everywhere): open books, graduation caps, lightbulbs, traditional education icons, cartoon illustrations. Emoji and photography are banned everywhere.
- **Iconography splits by surface** (owner's decision 2026-09-15, `DEC-079`, `docs/plan/16-ui-redesign.md` §4.2.1): the **marketing site** keeps only the eight permitted glyphs — dots, lines, chevron, arrow, check, spinner, close, plus. The **app and consoles** use a house-drawn icon set of ~28 glyphs, because icons reduce text density and make a 59-screen product friendlier. Three conditions hold: hand-authored inline SVG with **no icon-library dependency, ever**; one drawing spec (24px grid, 1.7px stroke, round caps/joins, built from the same circles and lines as the eight); and the forbidden imagery above still stands.
- Visual concept: the **Knowledge Network** — connected dots, thin silver lines, ripple effects, knowledge flowing between people. Dots may glow, pulse, and connect; they carry the entire visual language.
- Bilingual: Arabic RTL is primary and authored-first; English LTR mirrors it. Logical CSS properties only.

### Motion Personality (updated: "grand" direction approved by owner)
The owner asked for motion worthy of a media production group — production-logo stings, cinematic reveals, scroll-directed sequences. Motion should feel like **title design and cinematography** (racked focus, light sweeps, dolly moves, edit cuts), never like UI-library defaults (no bounce/elastic, no hover scale-ups, no confetti). Every grand moment needs a beautiful `prefers-reduced-motion` static state and must hold 60fps on mid-range phones.

### Design Principles
1. **One metaphor, everywhere**: every visual and motion idea must be expressible as dots, lines, and light.
2. **Cinema, not decoration**: motion mimics camera and edit grammar (focus pulls, sweeps, cuts, dollies) with exponential ease-outs.
3. **Generosity is the luxury signal**: space and restraint over ornament; when in doubt, remove.
4. **Arabic leads**: RTL is the source composition; English is the mirror, never the other way.
5. **Grand but graceful**: every showpiece degrades to a still that could hang in the deck — reduced motion, old devices, and no-JS users all get a complete experience.
