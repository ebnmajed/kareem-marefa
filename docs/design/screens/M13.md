### Attendee stories · `StoryAdd.dc.html` · `StoryAttendee.dc.html` · `AdminAttendance.dc.html`
**Owner's rulings, 2026-10-04: photos and video up to 15 s; posting until 24 h after the session.**
A checked-in attendee opens the session's story and taps «أضف»: a full-screen capture — tap for a
photo, hold for video (0:15 cap shown), gallery at the start, flip at the end, one caption line.
Posting creates a **photo** in the session's album (same points, same cap, EXIF stripped) **and** a
story frame; a video is a story frame only (no album entry, no points). The frame shows the
attendee's avatar, name, age, the caption, and `0:12` for video; members see it like any frame
(24 h, reactions). **Admin**: the session's الحضور tab gets a «قصص الحضور» strip — every attendee
frame, kept **forever** with the poster's ring and «أزل»; removal pulls it from the story and the
album. Reports and takedowns use the existing photo queues; «أزلني» applies to video too. Video:
≤ 15 s, ≤ 60 MB, transcoded to one MP4 rendition, served from the asset bucket, deleted with the
session. Gates: checked-in only; from session start to +24 h; the «أضف» control is absent otherwise.
