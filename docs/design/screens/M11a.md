## 0. The console frame

**Not the party.** `REQ-UIX-053`'s test stands: the console takes the palette, radii and type —
`h1` in the display face — and none of the motion, objects or stickers. Top bar 52 px: wordmark ·
«لوحة الإدارة» · org name · «التطبيق» · avatar. **Rail 220 px** at the inline-start, sticky, the
`admin.shell.nav.*` items in six ruled groups, queue counts as small coral badges. Content takes the
rest (≈1000 px), 24 px padding, `h1` row with the page's one primary action at its end. Under `lg`
the rail becomes a sheet behind ≡ and every table becomes a card stack (`042` phone shows it).

## 1. SCR-040 لوحة المؤسسة · `AdminDashboard.dc.html`

`h1` + month · **يحتاج انتباهك**: four tiles, count in coral display face, label, the oldest item's
age (`attention.oldestSince`); each tile is a link to its queue; the empty state is the one line
`attention.empty` · six `stat` tiles (`overview`: sessions this month, confirmed RSVPs, check-ins,
attendance rate, active members, points issued) · **مسار المقترحات** as one segmented bar with the
counts in its caption (`pipeline.*`) · **القادمة**: a `data-table` of the next sessions (title ·
date · presenter · reservations · status) · three top-lists (`topPresentersTitle`,
`topCategoriesTitle`, `topCompaniesTitle`; `topEmpty` when short). Counts are links
(`REQ-ORG-017`). No charts beyond the bar.

## 2. SCR-041 المقترحات · `AdminProposals.dc.html`

**Split view**: a 360 px list at the inline-start (filter chips `proposals.filters.*` with counts;
rows: title · proposer + company · age; current row bordered accent) and the detail beside it:
title · proposer · submitted date · status badge · «افتح كجلسة» (approved only) · chips
(category, level, duration) · abstract · presenters · preliminary materials · notes · a `details`
«تعديلات على المحتوى منذ الإرسال · N» holding the **content-edit diff** (`REQ-PRO-009`, `del`/`ins`
per field) · the decision card: one `textarea` «رسالة للمقترِح» + «اعتمد» (primary) · «اطلب تعديلًا»
· «ارفض» (text, coral). Keyboard: ↑↓ walks the list, Enter opens. Under `lg` the list is the page
and the detail is a route. Empty: `proposals.empty`.

## 3. SCR-042 الجلسات · `AdminSessions.dc.html` · `AdminSessionsPhone.dc.html`

The **`data-table`** pattern for the console: toolbar (search 280 px · filter chips with the
current value in the chip · count) · «جلسة جديدة» primary on the `h1` row · sticky header · row
checkboxes with «تحديد الكل» · columns العنوان · الحالة (`badge`, `DEC-073` tones) · الموعد ·
المكان · المُقدِّم (avatar with team ring + name) · الحجوزات («N / M · waitlist») · ⋯ row menu ·
footer «1 – 9 من 41» + السابقة/التالية. **Selection reveals a bulk bar** in place of the toolbar
(«N محدّدة · ألغِ · صدّر CSV · أغلق») — annotated, not drawn. Phone: the same rows as cards (title +
badge, date · venue, presenter, seats), the toolbar's chips in a scroll row, «جديدة» on the `h1`.
Sort by any column header; the default is date ascending with live first.

## 4. SCR-043 إعدادات الجلسة — الجدولة · `AdminSessionHub.dc.html`

`/app/admin/sessions/[id]/schedule`, the hub's first tab. Header: breadcrumb · `h1` title · status
badge · «صفحة الجلسة» · «ألغِ الجلسة» (quiet; the primary lifecycle action depends on state: «انشر»
for drafts, «ألغِ» for published, nothing for completed). **Tabs** الجدولة · المحتوى · الحضور ·
الاستبانة · الشهادات (`REQ-SES-020`; `DEC-178`'s redirect lands on الجدولة for admins and الحضور for
moderators). **Read mode by default** (`DEC-NEXT-23`): one key/value card — الموعد · المكان ·
السعة · إغلاق التسجيل · آخر إلغاء · المُقدِّمون · تسجيل الحضور · الشهادة — then «عدّل» (primary)
and «أعد الجدولة». Edit mode is the same card as a form with Save/Cancel (not drawn; `0084`'s
fields). Side column: reservations / waitlist / poster formats card, and the session's log. The
المحتوى tab (not drawn) holds title, abstract, objectives, tags, materials, tasks in the same
read/edit model.

## 5. SCR-044 إعدادات الجلسة — الحضور · `AdminAttendance.dc.html`

The الحضور tab, drawn live. Header as above with «شاشة التقديم» as the primary. A stats row
(محجوز · حاضر · المعدّل · بلا حجز · يدوي) and, beside it, the **code card**: the code in the display
face, the rotation countdown, «أبطل», the open/closed `switch`. Toolbar: chips الكل · حاضر · لم
يحضر, «تسجيل يدوي» (opens the member `combobox` + reason sheet, `REQ-CHK-008`), «CSV». Table:
العضو (avatar + name — **faces are allowed here**, `DEC-099`'s host placement) · الحجز (محجوز /
بلا حجز / قائمة انتظار) · وقت الحضور · الطريقة (رمز / يدوي · admin name) · الحالة · ⋯ (revoke
with reason → the ledger reversal). Completed sessions: the code card is replaced by the final rate;
rows are read-only except revoke.
