Companion to rows **E** and **F** of the canvas **كريم معرفة · M10a + M10b — Member screens** (8
artboards). Goes in the repo as `docs/design/screens/M10b.md`, beside `M10a.md`, whose §0 (the

`DECISIONS.md` win; deviations are `DEC-NEXT` proposals (§8); one artboard = one full state; the
other states are listed here; Western numerals (`DEC-124`); copy from `messages/ar/` where a string
exists, ★ marks a new string.

## 1. SCR-013 the viewer · `Viewer.dc.html` · `ViewerDesktop.dc.html`

Immersive, on **black**, not ink: the page is the only bright thing. Phone: chrome bar (close · title ·
«صفحة N من M» · download `icon-button` **only when `allow_download`** — a denied member gets no
button and no URL, `07 §6`) · the rendered page, whole, centred · bottom chrome: prev · page
`range` scrubber with «N من M» · next. **Tap toggles the chrome; swipe pages; pinch zooms.**

★ **The navigation model, not a mirrored icon** (`09` SCR-013): "next" advances in the reading
direction — leftwards in Arabic. The "next" control sits at the inline-end (left), is the accent
one, and its glyph points left; on desktop **← is next and → is previous** in RTL, Page Down/Up,
Home/End as usual. The thumbnail rail is at the **inline-start** (right), scrolls vertically,
current page outlined accent. The footer states «الملف الأصلي لا يُحمَّل في المتصفح؛ الصفحات مُصيَّرة
مسبقًا» — the source PDF is never fetched (`REQ-MAT-007`).

States: **loading** (page 1 eager, ±2 prefetched, a bone-coloured page skeleton) · **rendering**
(`materials.list.renderStatus.rendering`, centred on the black) · **Keynote / download-only**
(«هذا الملف متاح للتحميل فقط», `DEC-006`) · **failed** (retry + the PDF-upload guidance from
`materials.list.substitutionWarning` family) · **audio** materials never open here (they play inline
on `012`). No motion. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the page-slide.

## 2. SCR-015 rate · `Rate.dc.html`

Reachable only after check-in (the route 404s otherwise, `REQ-RAT-002`). Back · `h1`
`ratings.form.title` · session mini-row with «حضرت» · **two `star-input` rows**, 48 px stars, each a
`radiogroup` with per-star `radio`s and the count read back under the row («4 نجوم») · `textarea`
`ratings.form.commentLabel` with «N من 2000» · the **honest anonymity `panel`** —
`ratings.form.anonymityNotice` with `min` interpolated, **including** the org-admin exception
(D36, OQ-009) · bottom `action-bar`: `ratings.form.submit` + the window line
`ratings.form.windowClosesAt` + «يمكنك تعديله حتى ذلك الحين» ★.

★ **Stars fill from the right** (`09`): the row is a `dir="rtl"` flex row so star 1 is the
rightmost; selection fills rightmost-first; hover previews the same way. Keyboard: → decreases, ←
increases in RTL (the arrow follows the visual direction). States: **submitted** (the form stays
editable, submit reads `ratings.form.update`, `ratings.form.submitted` toast once) · **closed**
(`ratings.prompt.closed`, stars read-only, no bar) · **presenter** never sees this screen for their
own session. Moment: none. The rating award (`REQ-PTS` social points) lands via the ledger, and the
points count-up plays on the hub, not here.

## 3. SCR-017 propose · `Propose.dc.html`

Tab route: the `tab-bar` shows with اقترح raised **and current** (bone fill, not accent — the
raised tab has no "active" colour of its own, so current = bone). Regions: `h1`
`proposals.propose.title` + «مقترحاتي N» link → `018` list ★ · the lead in display face, accent
(`proposals.propose.lead`) · `proposals.propose.leadBody` · the **no-schedule `panel`**

محفوظة قبل …» ★ (autosave, `REQ-PRO-003`) · **section 1 الموضوع**: title `input` with
`titleHint` + `charCount` · abstract `textarea` · category `select` · level as **three segmented
`radio` chips** · audience `input` (اختياري) · duration `number` + `durationUnit` + `durationHint` ·
**section 2 المُقدِّمون والملاحظات**: co-presenters `combobox` with chips (team ring in the chip),
`coPresentersHint` + `coPresentersLimit` · notes `textarea` · the dashed `materialsNote` row · an
earn `panel` («+100 للتقديم، تُدفع عند اكتمال جلستك. وشارة «مُقدِّم» مع أول جلسة» ★, values from the
scoring catalogue, never hard-coded) · sticky `action-bar`: `form.submit` + `form.saveDraft`.

★ **No date, time or venue field exists** — not hidden, absent, in the form and in the schema
(`REQ-PRO-001`). States: **error summary** (`errorSummaryTitle` + `errorSummaryCount`, focus moves
to it, `route-error` shape) · **hosting gated** (`REQ-REC-008`: the form is replaced by a card
naming what would qualify them, with the level ramp) · **co-presenter limit reached** (the
combobox disables with `coPresentersLimit`) · **resubmit** (same form prefilled, submit reads
`form.resubmit`, the admin's reason pinned above section 1). Success: `created.submittedTitle` /
`created.submittedBody` as a full-screen `empty-state`-shaped confirmation with «افتح مقترحك» → `018`.
No moment — the proposal is not an award.

## 4. SCR-018 my proposal · `Proposal.dc.html`

Drawn in its richest state, **changes requested**. Back · `h1` «مقترحي» + submitted date · a
five-step `stepper` (أُرسل · قيد المراجعة · طُلب تعديل · معتمد · مُجدوَل), the current step coral ·
the **admin's reason `card`** bordered coral: admin avatar (HQ ring), name, role, time, the reason
as a `blockquote`, then the one primary «عدّل وأعد الإرسال» → `017` in resubmit state · the
read-only summary (title as `h2`, chips: category · level · duration, abstract) · **المُقدِّمون**
managed here (`form.presentersElsewhere`): rows with reply state pills («قبلت» accent / «بانتظار الرد»
muted / «اعتذر» ★), remove control on pending ones, «+ أضف مُقدِّمًا مشاركًا» · **مواد مبدئية**: PDF rows
+ dashed «أرفق ملفًا (PDF)» — PDF only (`DEC-058`) · **السجل** (dated events) · «اسحب المقترح» as a
quiet link, confirm in a `sheet` · bottom `action-bar` mirrors the primary.

Other states: **submitted / in review** (steps 1–2 done, no reason card, primary absent, «يمكنك
التعديل حتى يبدأ المشرف المراجعة» ★) · **approved** (step 4 accent, «بانتظار الجدولة»; the session
appears when the admin schedules it, with a link) · **scheduled** (step 5; the card becomes the
session's poster + «افتح الجلسة») · **rejected** (the reason card in muted, no primary, «اقترح
موضوعًا آخر») · **withdrawn** (grey, read-only). The **list** at `/app/propose` («مقترحاتي») is a
short `row` list above the form when the member has any — no separate screen.

## 8. Deltas vs `docs/plan` — proposed decisions

- **`DEC-NEXT-17` — the viewer's ground is black, not ink**, the only surface in the app that is;
  the page must be the brightest thing on screen. `00-direction.md` lists ink as the ground; this is
  a named exception, like the print surfaces.
- **`DEC-NEXT-18` — «مقترحاتي» is a list on `/app/propose`, not a route.** `09` places `018` at
  `/app/propose/[id]` only; the list of one's own proposals had no home. Above the form when non-empty.
- **The level badge appears in the directory** (`REQ-PRF-005` lists name, title, company). Levels are
  public standing (`A33` tier 1 includes المستوى), so the badge is within the tier; noted so nobody
  reads it as a leak.
- Unchanged and worth restating for the lead: no photo tagging anywhere (`09` SCR-020 note); the
  source PDF is never fetched (`REQ-MAT-007`); the anonymity notice includes the admin exception.
